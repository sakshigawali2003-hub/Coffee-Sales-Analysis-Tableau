Coffee Quality and Market Analysis – Tableau

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee quality, pricing, species, processing methods and countries of origin using Tableau.

The dataset contains 35,700 coffee records across 7 countries. The main objective is to understand coffee quality and market price variations and generate meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and Tableau Story.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total coffee records.
- Compare coffee prices across different countries.
- Analyze average coffee quality scores.
- Compare Arabica and Robusta coffee species.
- Explore different coffee processing methods.
- Analyze average prices by quality grade.
- Understand coffee origin and market value.
- Examine record classifications and data reliability.
- Generate useful business insights and recommendations.

🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- Tableau
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Calculated Fields
- Data Visualization
- Tableau Story

📈 Dashboard Analysis

1. Coffee Overview Dashboard

Provides an overview of total coffee records, average coffee price, average quality score and countries of origin.

2. Average Price by Country

Compares coffee prices across Kenya, Ethiopia, Colombia, Honduras, Brazil, Indonesia and Vietnam.

3. Coffee Species and Sensory Profile

Compares Arabica and Robusta coffee species based on sensory characteristics and quality scores.

4. Coffee Quality by Processing

Analyzes coffee quality across different processing methods.

5. Average Price by Quality Grade

Compares coffee prices across different quality grades.

6. Origin and Market Value

Analyzes coffee origin and market value across different countries.

7. Record Type Analysis

Examines record classifications and dataset metadata.

8. Temporal Split Analysis

Explores temporal lot assignments and time-related information.

9. Data Structure and Reliability

Examines dataset structure and data reliability.

10. Business Insights

Presents important findings and recommendations based on the analysis.

💡 Key Business Insights

- The dataset contains 35,700 coffee records.
- The average coffee quality score is 77.60.
- The average coffee price is 4.813.
- The analysis covers 7 countries of origin.
- Kenya has the highest displayed average price of 6.849.
- Ethiopia has an average price of 6.059.
- Vietnam has the lowest displayed average price of 2.680.
- Arabica coffee has a higher average quality score than Robusta in this analysis.
- Coffee prices vary across different countries of origin.

📌 Business Recommendations

- Focus on coffee types with higher quality scores.
- Compare prices across countries before purchasing.
- Identify processing methods associated with better quality.
- Review duplicate records and revisions.
- Maintain reliable data for better business decisions.
